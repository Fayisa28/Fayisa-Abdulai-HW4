# Campus Customs Design Changes

The storefront now uses a calm Yale boutique language: Yale Blue anchors the interface, white surfaces keep merchandise prominent, and heritage gray dividers create a crisp rhythm. This familiar visual system builds trust quickly and makes the shop feel worth exploring.

- **Collegiate typography:** Serif headings establish campus character while a clean sans-serif keeps product details easy to scan. Strong hierarchy helps shoppers understand products faster and stay in the browse flow.
- **Editorial product cards:** Centered catalogue photography, balanced padding, soft shadows, clear prices, and short descriptions make each item feel curated. Better scanning encourages more product discovery and comparison.
- **Gentle motion:** Cards reveal with a quiet fade and lift smoothly on hover; the chat dock slides up gracefully. Responsive feedback makes browsing feel polished and inviting, encouraging longer sessions without distraction.
- **Consistent detail pages:** Every card opens the same branded view with a large image, full description, exact price, colors, sizes, and live stock. Confidence in the information reduces hesitation at the purchase decision.
- **Persistent navigation:** The sticky Yale-blue header keeps Products, About Us, account actions, and Home within reach. Easy movement supports continued browsing and makes returning to merchandise effortless.
- **Helpful chat dock:** The compact rounded assistant is always available without taking over the page. Product-aware search returns real cards and direct paths to details, turning questions into additional merchandise discovery.
- **Accessible restraint:** Focus states, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support make the same premium experience comfortable across devices and preferences.

Together, these changes make Campus Customs feel trustworthy, distinctive, and easy to browse—qualities that help shoppers stay engaged and move confidently toward a purchase.
